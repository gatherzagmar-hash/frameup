How to upload so TikTok ruins it as little as possible
  1. Upload through tiktok.com on a computer, not the app.
  2. In the app, turn on high-quality uploads (Upload HD) and turn off Data Saver.
  3. Upload on good Wi-Fi. A weak connection can lower the uploaded quality.
  4. Always upload the original file from your editor, never one downloaded from TikTok or Insta.
  5. Export from your editor at a high bitrate. The better the input, the better the result.
TikTok compresses every video anyway. This method hands it the cleanest possible file, but nobody can honestly promise zero compression.
